Account & Data Deletion Request

This page allows users of Quiz IQ to request deletion of their account and associated data.

If you signed in using Google Sign-In, your account is linked to your Google email address.

What will be deleted:

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile data
  • Quiz progress and scores
  • Any stored user activity data

Request deletion

Requests are processed within 7–30 days. You may also delete your account directly inside the app.
