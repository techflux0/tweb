Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 1, 2024

Quiz IQ ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture. This is used to create and identify your game account.

b) Game Data

We collect your game progress including: scores, levels completed, categories unlocked, coins and gems balance, quiz history, and leaderboard rankings.

c) Device Information

We may collect device information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and improving app performance.

d) Purchase Information

Through Google Play Billing, we receive purchase confirmation but do not have access to your full payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your game account
  • Save and sync your game progress across devices
  • Display leaderboard rankings to other players
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Send important app updates and notifications
  • Serve relevant advertisements

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database, Analytics - Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising - Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases - Google Play Terms
🔐 Data Storage: Your data is stored securely on Google Cloud Firebase servers. We retain your data as long as you maintain an active account.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • With third-party services as described above
  • Your name and score appear on public leaderboards

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of analytics (device settings)
  • Reset advertising ID (device settings)

To delete your account, contact us at quiziq.support@gmail.com. Your data will be removed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Quiz IQ is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with data, please contact us.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where our servers are located (United States). By using Quiz IQ, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use after changes means acceptance.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

📧 Email: quiziq.support@gmail.com

💬 In-app: Settings → Contact Support

📋 Data Deletion Request: To request account deletion, send an email from your registered email address with subject "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" to quiziq.support@gmail.com.
